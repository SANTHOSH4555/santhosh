1. *Add a Task*:  
   Begin by entering a task title into the designated input field. Once you've typed in your task, you have two options to add it to your list. You can either click the "Add" button, prominently located next to the input field, or simply press the "Enter" key on your keyboard. This action will seamlessly add the task to your ongoing list.

2. *Complete a Task*:  
   To mark a task as complete, locate the checkbox situated next to the task you wish to complete. Clicking this box will instantly update the task’s status to 'completed'. This action visually signals the completion by either striking through the task name or moving it to a separate 'completed tasks' section, depending on your interface settings.

3. *Edit a Task*:  
   If you need to make changes to an existing task, you have the flexibility to do so. Simply click on the task title itself or opt for an "Edit" button if available. This will allow you to enter an editable field where you can modify the task's details as needed. Once your edits are made, don't forget to save the changes by clicking the "Save" button to ensure your updates are kept.

4. *Delete a Task*:  
   Should you need to remove a task altogether, find the "Delete" button or icon located adjacent to the task in question. Clicking this button will prompt the task to be permanently removed from your list, helping you maintain a clutter-free task environment. 

5. *Filter Tasks*:  
   To efficiently manage and view your tasks based on their status, utilize the filter buttons typically labeled as "All," "Completed," and "Pending." By selecting one of these options, you can easily switch between viewing all your tasks, only those that are completed, or just the pending tasks that still require your attention. This feature allows for focused task management and streamlined workflow.
